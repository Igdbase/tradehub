# TradeHub — Outreach Templates

Reusable starting points for the cold-outreach channel in the marketing blueprint. Personalize every bracket before sending — a template that's obviously copy-pasted is worse than no message at all.

**Before sending any of these:**
- Reference something specific and real (a recent post, a call, a course topic) — the bracket isn't optional filler.
- Keep DMs under ~500 characters. X/Twitter and Instagram both bury longer first-messages, and links in a first DM often get filtered — save the landing page link for the reply, not the opener.
- Lead with the thing they haven't thought about (the prop-firm angle), not with "I built a SaaS platform."

---

## Cold DM — by segment

**The Telegram Signal Seller**
> Hey [Name] — saw your [pair] call on [channel name] this week, solid setup. Quick one: are you still collecting payments manually through bank transfers? I'm building a platform that handles that automatically — your Telegram stays exactly as is, but you get billing, a course area, and a journal your students can see, all under your own name. Worth a 15-min call?

**The Course-Only Educator**
> Hey [Name] — been through some of your [topic] lessons, really clear teaching style. Are you running anything recurring right now, or mostly one-off course sales? I'm building a platform made specifically for trading educators — tiers, locked progression, real completion data — under your own brand, not a generic course site. Open to a quick call?

**The Prop-Firm Mentor**
> Hey [Name] — quick question most prop-firm coaches don't get asked: if a funded student copies one of your calls and it's flagged as "external signal use," that's their account gone. I'm building a platform designed around exactly that — alerts for funded accounts, auto-copy only for personal ones — plus courses and a journal under your brand. Worth 15 minutes?

**The Crypto Trader**
> Hey [Name] — your [pair/asset] trades have been worth following. Ever thought about letting your audience auto-copy your live trades instead of just watching? I'm building a platform that does exactly that for Binance/Bybit, plus a course area and journal, all under your own brand. Want to see how it works?

## Ultra-short opener
For platforms where longer first-messages land in a "requests" folder and need to earn a reply before anyone reads more than one line:

> Hey [Name] — built something for trading educators, think it could be useful for you. Got 2 minutes for a quick question?

## Email (when a business inquiries address is available)
Email tolerates more length and an actual link, since it isn't filtered the way DM platforms filter first contact.

**Subject:** A platform built around the gap most signal sellers don't see

> Hi [Name],
>
> Followed your [channel/page] for a while. Most signal platforms don't distinguish between personal and funded accounts — which is exactly the kind of gap that gets prop-firm students banned for "external copying." I built TradeHub specifically around that distinction: students on personal accounts can auto-copy your calls, students on funded accounts get a clean alert instead, so they keep their pass.
>
> Beyond signals, you get a fully branded course area and a trading journal — all under your name, nothing visibly TradeHub. No subscription fee; we take a share only of what your students pay you, and you set your own pricing.
>
> If that's worth a look: [landing page link]
>
> Happy to do a 15-minute call if useful.
>
> [Your name]

## Follow-up (5–7 days, no response)

> Hey [Name] — following up in case this got buried. No pressure either way, but if a 15-minute call about [protecting your funded-account students / getting your courses off WhatsApp] sounds useful, I'm around this week. If not, no worries at all.
