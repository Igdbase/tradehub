# TradeHub — Influencer Acquisition Marketing Blueprint

**Focus:** Getting trading influencers (forex & crypto) to adopt TradeHub
**Customer:** The influencer, not the student — students are recruited by the influencer, not by you
**Initial market:** Nigeria-anchored, Paystack-rail markets (expandable across anglophone Africa)

---

## 1. Positioning

**Elevator pitch:**
TradeHub gives trading educators a fully-branded course, signals, and journal platform they can launch in days — with no code, no exposure to prop-firm-banning auto-trades, and no cost until their own students pay them first.

**Tagline options:**
- "Your brand. Your students. Your platform — they never see ours."
- "Built so your funded-account students don't get banned."
- "The platform that disappears so your brand doesn't have to."

**The one differentiator to lead with:** almost every competitor either bolts signals onto a generic course platform or auto-executes signals with no regard for prop-firm Terms of Service — which gets students' funded accounts terminated. TradeHub is built around the Signal Alerts / Auto-Copy split specifically to protect influencers' prop-firm students. That's a real, defensible, non-obvious selling point. Lead outreach with it — it signals you understand their world, not just SaaS.

**Solana partner/payment note:** Solana can appear as an optional payment rail and partner credibility cue ("Solana Pay / USDC checkout available for crypto-first audiences"). Do not make it the headline unless the page is specifically for a Solana audience. The core promise remains: your branded trading education platform, Paystack/local payments by default, Solana checkout when it helps the influencer or investor story.

## 2. Who You're Selling To

| Segment | Profile | Core pain today |
|---|---|---|
| **The Telegram Signal Seller** | Already monetizing via Telegram/WhatsApp + manual bank transfer | Piracy, manual collection, chargebacks, no structure — looks unprofessional next to bigger names |
| **The Course-Only Educator** | YouTube/IG following, sells one-off courses | No recurring revenue, no idea who's actually watching or finishing |
| **The Prop-Firm Mentor** | Coaches students through funded-account challenges | Highest legal/reputational exposure — one careless "copy this" post can get students' accounts terminated |
| **The Crypto Trader** | Personal live accounts, audience wants to mirror trades | Wants real automated execution, not just alerts — this ships at MVP for Binance/Bybit |

Use the vetting requirement (identity + track record/audience proof) as a *feature*, not a *gate* — "we only onboard influencers who can prove a real audience" is itself a trust signal you can put in outreach copy.

---

## 3. Messaging Pillars

1. **Speed without engineering** — "Own a professional platform in days, not months." No dev hire, no Bubble/Webflow hack job.
2. **You keep the brand, we keep the lights on** — full white-label; nothing visibly says TradeHub.
3. **Pay only when you earn** — no platform subscription fee; Paystack splits automatically (90/10 default, negotiable), settled independently to both parties.
4. **Compliance built in, not bolted on** — Signal Alerts vs. Auto-Copy routing, prop-firm disclaimers, risk disclosure flow — protects the influencer's reputation as much as the student's account.
5. **You see what's actually working** — revenue dashboard, churn, completion rates, aggregate journal stats. Most Telegram-based educators have none of this today.

**Guardrail for every piece of marketing you write (to influencers, and what you coach influencers to say to students):** never imply guaranteed returns, predictable income, or "get rich" framing. The Influencer Code of Conduct bans this on their end — your own acquisition marketing should model the same restraint. It's also a credibility advantage: a platform that doesn't hype itself reads as more serious to a serious educator.

## 4. Competitive Frame

| | DIY Telegram + bank transfer | Generic course platforms (Kajabi/Teachable-style) | Generic signal bots | TradeHub |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White-label branding | N/A | Partial | No | **Full** |
| Local payment rails (Naira/Paystack) | Manual | No | No | **Native** |
| Course + Signals + Journal in one | No | Course only | Signals only | **All three** |
| Prop-firm ToS-aware signal routing | No | N/A | No | **Yes** |
| Cost to start | Free but manual labor | Monthly subscription | Monthly subscription | **Setup fee, then % only on what they earn** |

Don't disparage competitors by name in outreach — frame it as "what most educators are stitching together today" and let the gaps speak for themselves.

## 5. Acquisition Funnel

```
Discover (DM / content / community)
   ↓
Landing page → "Apply for a workspace"
   ↓
Vetting call (identity + track record/audience proof)
   ↓
Manual approval + setup fee
   ↓
Guided onboarding (Branding → Code of Conduct → Telegram bot → Pricing → First course)
   ↓
Activation = first paying student
   ↓
Case study / testimonial / referral
```

Because onboarding is manual at MVP, your funnel is intentionally narrow and high-touch — optimize early channels for *quality of conversation*, not volume.

## 6. Channel Plan

| Channel | Cost | Effort | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Direct outreach** (X/Twitter, IG DMs, YouTube comments) | Time only | High | Now — this is how you get pilot cohort #1–5 |
| **Trading communities** (Discord servers, prop-firm forums, FX groups) | Low | Medium | Now, in parallel — go where signal sellers already gather |
| **Content marketing** (threads on the prop-firm ToS risk, "what happens when a signal seller gets a student banned") | Time | Medium | Start immediately — this is your authority wedge, and it's evergreen |
| **In-person meetups/expos** (Lagos/Abuja forex & crypto meetups) | Travel/ticket | Medium | Once you have 1–2 case studies to show in person |
| **Referral from onboarded influencers** | Low (incentive) | Low | As soon as pilot cohort sees results — don't wait for the formal Phase 3 affiliate program to ask informally |
| **Paid social ads** | $ | Low | Defer until you have 2–3 verifiable case studies — paying to acquire influencers with zero proof is the most expensive way to learn what doesn't work |

## 7. Sample Outreach (template — personalize every send)

> Hey [Name] — been following your [forex/crypto] content, especially [specific recent post/signal]. Quick question: have you thought about what happens if a funded-account student auto-copies one of your calls and gets banned for "group trading"? I'm building a platform that's specifically designed around that risk — plus gives you your own branded app for courses, signals, and a trading journal, with payments split automatically so you're only paid once your students pay you. Worth a 15-minute call?

**Why this works:** opens with specific proof you actually follow them (not a copy-paste blast), leads with the non-obvious compliance hook instead of generic SaaS pitch, and closes with a low-friction ask.

**Landing page headline options:**
- "Your platform. Your brand. Your students never see ours."
- "Stop losing students to bank transfers and screenshots."
- "Built so your funded-account students don't get banned."

## 8. Pilot Cohort Strategy

Target **3–5 influencers across all four segments** in the first 30–45 days. Offer one of:
- Reduced platform split (e.g., 5% instead of 10%) for the first 90 days, or
- Waived setup fee

in exchange for a testimonial, a usable case study (revenue, student count, or engagement numbers), and an introduction to one peer educator. This is the cheapest, highest-trust marketing asset you can produce — real numbers from a real Nigerian trading educator beat any ad copy.

---

## 9. 90-Day Roadmap

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Build** | 1–15 | Outreach list (50–100 targets), landing page/one-pager live, vetting checklist finalized, demo script ready |
| **Open** | 16–30 | Run outreach, book vetting calls, close first 2–3 pilot influencers |
| **Prove** | 31–60 | Onboard remaining pilot cohort (5–10 total), collect usage data, produce first case study |
| **Compound** | 61–90 | Layer in community/partnership channel, ask pilot cohort for referrals, start content marketing using real proof points, evaluate paid spend only once CAC payback is evident |

---

## 10. KPIs to Track

- Outreach sent → call booked rate
- Call booked → vetting-approved rate
- Approved → activated (first paying student) rate, and **time** to activation
- Average time from "workspace created" to "first revenue"
- Pilot cohort revenue trend (your 10% share)
- Testimonial/case-study yield per pilot influencer
- Referral rate from onboarded influencers
