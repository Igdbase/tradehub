The problem

Most trading education still runs on Telegram and a bank-transfer screenshot.

Manual, and a little fragile

Bank transfers, WhatsApp confirmations, chasing renewals by hand. One refund dispute turns into a week of back-and-forth.

Built for courses, not for trading

Generic course platforms host video fine. They have no signal delivery, no journal, and no idea what a funded account is.

One mistake from a banned account

Auto-executing a signal into a prop-firm account is exactly the kind of "external trade copying" most firms terminate accounts for.

What your students get

One app. Your name on all of it.

Course Hub

Lessons play inside your app — never a YouTube link. Progress tracking, locked chapters, and quizzes keep students moving instead of dropping off.

Trade Signals

Post once. Personal accounts can auto-copy the trade; funded accounts get an alert instead — so a prop-firm rule never costs a student their account.

Trading Journal

Every trade logs itself from their exchange. They see what's working. You see who's actually trading, not just watching.

Why this matters

Most platforms don't know the difference. We built around it.

Prop-firm terms of service almost universally ban external trade copying — and most signal tools have no idea, because they treat every account the same way.

TradeHub routes the two cases differently from the ground up. A personal account can auto-copy a signal the moment it's posted. A funded account gets a clean alert instead, so the student decides — and keeps their pass.
